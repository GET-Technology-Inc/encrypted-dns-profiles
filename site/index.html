安裝方式 / How to install

  1. 在 iPhone 或 iPad 上用 Safari 點選上方的下載連結，允許下載描述檔。
    Open this page in Safari on your iPhone or iPad and tap a download link, then allow the profile download.
  2. 前往「設定」，點最上方的「已下載描述檔」，確認簽署者顯示為已驗證後安裝。
    Go to Settings, tap "Profile Downloaded" at the top, check that the signer shows as Verified, then install.
  3. 要換供應商就安裝另一份，舊的會自動被取代；要恢復預設就到「設定 › 一般 › VPN 與裝置管理」移除描述檔。
    Installing another profile replaces the previous one. Remove the profile under Settings › General › VPN & Device Management to go back to the default resolver.

描述檔只設定 DNS 解析器，不會安裝憑證、VPN 或任何管理設定。原始碼與產生方式公開於 GitHub。
These profiles only set the DNS resolver. No certificates, VPNs or management payloads are installed. Source and build pipeline are on GitHub.
